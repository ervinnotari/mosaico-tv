## Video codec patents

H.264/AVC and H.265/HEVC are covered by patents in many countries. Cisco's
royalty-free patent license for OpenH264 applies only to the binaries that
Cisco distributes; this project compiles OpenH264 from source, so that license
does not apply. H.265 is decoded by the TV's own hardware. Anyone distributing
this software commercially should evaluate the applicable patent licensing.
This notice is informational and is not legal advice.
