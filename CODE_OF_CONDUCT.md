# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and focus on
the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not
accepted in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behavior to mosaicotv.talk@outlook.com. Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, close
discussions or block participants who do not follow this code.
